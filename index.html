Experimental Quantum Physicist

I build and automate experimental quantum and photonic hardware, from optical and RF systems to cryogenic measurement platforms and quantitative device characterization.

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Physics at the University of California, Santa Barbara, with more than five years of experience in quantum hardware engineering and applied device physics.

My work spans experiment design, fiber and free-space optics, RF and electronic instrumentation, cryogenic integration, measurement automation, modeling, troubleshooting, and analysis. I am most at home where device physics meets the practical work of making a complex experiment reliable.
