Selected research projects and peer-reviewed work spanning diamond optomechanics, quantum sensing, phononics, and experimental automation.

Diamond optomechanics

High-Q optomechanical devices in the quantum regime

Designed and executed cryogenic characterization of diamond optomechanical devices, measuring mechanical quality factors greater than 10 million and identifying optical-absorption-induced heating as a performance-limiting mechanism.

Qm > 10⁷GHz mechanicsCryogenic characterization

Cryogenic quantum measurement

Ground-state operation and direct thermometry

Built and commissioned a dilution-refrigerator measurement platform integrating cryogenic optical, RF, DC, and positioning hardware, and demonstrated ground-state operation of GHz-frequency diamond optomechanical devices.

<250 mK platformDirect thermometry

Experimental software

Modular automation for complex experiments

Developed a Python framework integrating lasers, RF/microwave electronics, DAQs, cryogenic instrumentation, and optical hardware for closed-loop control, synchronized acquisition, automated characterization, and parameter extraction.

PythonInstrument APIsClosed-loop control

NV-center magnetometry

Automated 3D magnetic-field control and ODMR

Designed hardware/software control for a custom vector electromagnet and automated a 3D field-sweep protocol to determine crystallographic orientations of individual NV centers from ODMR measurements.
